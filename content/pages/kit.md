The short list. Things bought once, used daily, and never regretted. Prices are approximate and in Australian dollars; none of these links pays us.

## Watches

**Cartier Tank Louis Cartier, large, in yellow or rose gold** — The quintessential gentleman's watch, and the cornerstone of a wardrobe before it is the cornerstone of a collection. Less jewellery than essential hardware of being a grown-up. It doesn't say *look at me*; it says *I know who I am, and I'm getting on with life*. On this the house is not neutral. *AUD 20,000 · [cartier.com](https://www.cartier.com/watches/tank)*

**Rolex Submariner Date 126610LN** — The current production Sub. 41mm, ceramic bezel, 70-hour power reserve. Still the benchmark. Bought once, worn daily, never regretted. *AUD 15,350 · [rolex.com](https://www.rolex.com/watches/submariner)*

**Cartier Tank Must** — The watch that looks good on every wrist in every room. Steel case, blue hands, leather strap. Dress it up or down. A century of good design at a fraction of the Louis. *AUD 5,200 · [cartier.com](https://www.cartier.com/watches/tank)*

## Writing

**Montblanc Meisterstück 149** — The benchmark fountain pen. Heavy, balanced, and the nib breaks in to your hand over years. One purchase, decades of use. *AUD 1,290 · [montblanc.com](https://www.montblanc.com/meisterstuck-149)*

**Pilot Custom 823 (Amber)** — Vacuum fill, 14k nib, enormous ink capacity. The serious writer's pen. Half the price of the 149 and arguably a better writer out of the box. *AUD 490 · [pilotpen.com.au](https://www.pilotpen.com.au)*

**Stalogy 365 Notebook (A5, dot grid)** — Thin Tomoe River-style paper in a slim, undated daily format. The dot grid sits just below the surface — enough structure to keep your lines straight, invisible enough to let you sketch and scrawl. One of the few notebooks that rewards a fountain pen. *AUD 45*

## Photography

**Leica Q3 43** — Fixed 43mm lens. Full-frame sensor. The camera that goes out the door with you. No lens to choose; no excuses left. *AUD 9,600 · [leica-camera.com](https://leica-camera.com/q3-43)*

**Leica M11** — The rangefinder. Manual focus, deliberate photography. Not for everyone — for the people who want to slow down and see. *AUD 13,500 · [leica-camera.com](https://leica-camera.com/m11)*
