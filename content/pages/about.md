---
title: "About this site"
---

Tusenbruk is an independent publication about the relationship between a person and the objects they use — watches, cameras, pens, cars, luggage. Not reviews. Portraits.

Nothing to pay. No accounts, no newsletter capture, no data harvesting. This is a project about a common interest, published because the pieces deserve a page.

The objects covered here share grease and altitude and deadline and rain. They accumulate evidence. They become, eventually, irreplaceable — not because of what they're worth, but because of what they've been through.

*Earn the wear.*
