---
title: "After the wanting"
---

Tusenbruk begins after the wanting.

New objects arrive with specifications, prices and promises. None of those says much about the life the object will enter. That comes later: the corner worn smooth, the repair postponed, the irritation accepted because the rest still works.

These are portraits of people, seen through the things they use. A watch, camera, pen, notebook, bag or car earns a place here only when removing the person would remove the story.

Price is beside the point. Craft matters, but use is the proof. What works matters. So does what grates, what has broken, what was repaired and the detail no catalogue could know.

There are no scores, rankings, affiliate links or condition reports. The photographs show objects in use, or after use. The writing stops before admiration becomes worship.

Tusenbruk is a coined name: a thousand uses; something used a thousand times.

What remains is the point.

*Earn the wear.*
